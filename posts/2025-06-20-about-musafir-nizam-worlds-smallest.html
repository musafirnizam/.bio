Who is Musafir Nizam?

The World’s Smallest Traveller & Hitchhiker

Musafir Nizam is proudly known as the World’s Smallest Traveller & Hitchhiker, standing at just 116 cm (3.10 ft). But don’t let the size fool you — his vision is Himalayan, and his journey is nothing short of legendary.

From Delhi’s chaotic lanes to Ladakh’s frozen highways, Musafir Nizam has hitchhiked over 3000+ km across 13 Indian states and 4 Union Territories, without paid transport, relying solely on human kindness, courage, and grit.

🛣️ A Mission Beyond Travel

In 2025, he launched his most iconic journey yet — the Bharat Ekta Bhraman — a powerful solo travel mission to spread the message of Unity in Diversity.

"To connect people, cultures, and communities through pure human interaction — without filters, without money, without fear."
