FAQ

Who is Musafir Nizam?
Musafir Nizam is a simple, ordinary guy with no superpowers, an Indian solo hitchhiker, content creator and psychology student. Standing at 116 cm (3 feet 10 inches), he is recognized as the World's Smallest Traveller.
Why is he called the World's Smallest Traveller?
At 116 cm there is no recorded record of any solo hitchhiker of this height covering thousands of kilometres across India on his own.
What is hitchhiking?
Hitchhiking is asking everyday strangers for a lift without booking commercial tickets. For Nizam it is trust and the real heartbeat of the country.
What is Bharat Ekta Bhraman?
An independent journey to prove this entire country belongs to each of us equally. Ek Zameen, Ek Vatavaran, Ek Bharat.
